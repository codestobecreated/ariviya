*Company Overview*
Ariviya Deep Tech Pvt. Ltd., based in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, is an emerging deep-tech biotechnology startup founded in 2021 by scientists and entrepreneurs. It aims to translate locally developed scientific innovations into affordable, sustainable animal healthcare solutions. The company integrates nanotechnology, biotechnology, and polymer science to develop products for animal health, wound management, and antimicrobial protection.
Ariviya’s flagship innovation, MammaryO, is a nano-bio post-milking teat-dip solution designed to help prevent bovine Mastitis—a major threat to dairy productivity, milk quality, and farm economics. Company-reported studies show broad-spectrum activity against key mastitis-associated microorganisms and promising field performance.
Ariviya Deep Tech exemplifies how advanced science can emerge from a rural ecosystem and translate into socially relevant, commercially scalable innovations.
By combining NaxPoly technology, the company aims to develop sustainable, affordable, and evidence-based healthcare products that protect animals, improve farmers' livelihoods, support clean milk production, and ultimately improve human and environmental health.
